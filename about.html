Latter-day Lion

Too much of the conversation surrounding faith, particularly the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, happens in extremes.

On the one hand, you have shallow criticisms built on the flimsiest straw men that the lightest of breezes knocks them over.

On the other, cringe apologetics that are more likely to trigger a gag reflex than thoughtful reasoning and discussion.

And that says nothing about the channels that are being used as a replacement for melatonin.

That's where Monty comes in as the Latter-day Lion.

Not as a form of bravado, but posture.

A lion doesn't run from confrontation or attack blindly.

This exists to provide rigorous, no-nonsense analysis of theology, history, and modern religious claims with the wit of C.S. Lewis and the bite of (insert favorite political commentator here), with a focus on how Latter-day Saint criticisms stand up when placed under pressure.

Not straw men.

Not curation.

Real pressure.

Not by assuming faith exempts ideas from examination. Or that skepticism makes the argument correct.

Every claim is open to challenge. After all, sunlight is the best disinfectant.

After all, if a belief is true, it can withstand scrutiny when properly represented.

If not, it deserves to be questioned and mocked.

What Monty Does:

Engaging directly with:
  • Protestant, Catholic, Orthodox, and Reformed theological claims
  • Popular anti-Mormon arguments and assumptions
  • Modern faith-deconstruction narratives
  • Misrepresentations of early Christian history and doctrine based on the latest scholarship
This can happen in multiple forms:
  • Long-form theological breakdowns
  • Live debates and panel discussions
  • Real time conversations with critics and believers alike
  • Short-form responses that cut through the noise and get to the point
How:
  • Scripture read in context, not soundbites
  • History treated as history, not propaganda
  • Theology is evaluated by its internal consistency and its consequences
